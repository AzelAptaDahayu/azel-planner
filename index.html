How to study with active recall

Active recall means you learn by pulling ideas out of your head, not by reading again and again. Reading feels easy but you forget fast. Recalling feels hard, and that hard feeling is the learning.

  1. Put your phone in another room, or face down on Do Not Disturb.
  2. Sit at a desk or table, not on your bed. Start a 15-minute block.
  3. Read one small part only.
  4. Close it. Write what you remember on blank paper, or try the problem without looking.
  5. Open it, check, and fix your mistakes in another color.
  6. Take a 5-minute break. Stand up, drink water. No scrolling.

Review the same topic again after 1 day, 3 days, and 1 week. Study sets in Courses make this easy.
